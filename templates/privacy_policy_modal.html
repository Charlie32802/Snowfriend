Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Snowfriend. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, supportive environment for mental wellness and personal reflection. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Important: Snowfriend is an AI-powered companion, not a substitute for professional mental health care. For emergencies or serious concerns, please contact qualified professionals or crisis services.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Full name (required for account creation)
  • Email address (for account login, password recovery, and account notifications)
  • Username (automatically generated from email)
  • Password (encrypted using Argon2 secure hashing)
  • Account creation and last login timestamps

Conversation Data

  • Messages you send to Snowfriend
  • AI-generated responses from Snowfriend
  • Conversation timestamps
  • Topics discussed (stored for conversation continuity)
  • Your first name (used to personalize responses)

Media Search Data

  • Search queries when you request videos or images (e.g., "stress management tips")
  • Media content preferences and topics of interest
  • Timestamps of media searches
  • Note: Your name and email are NOT sent to media providers

Security & Usage Information

  • Login timestamps and authentication attempts
  • Failed login attempts (tracked for security)
  • Session activity and duration
  • Message count and rate limits (15 messages per day with hourly reset)
  • IP address (hashed with SHA-256 + salt for rate limiting)
  • Timezone information (for time-aware responses)
  • Password change history (last 3 password hashes - encrypted)

Feedback & Support

  • Anonymous feedback submissions (ratings, comments)
  • Optional images attached to feedback (metadata removed, max 5MB)
  • Hashed IP address (for spam prevention - SHA-256)
  • Submission timestamps
  • No personally identifiable information required for feedback

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide our service: Enable conversations, maintain context, and personalize responses using your first name
  • Search for helpful content: Use your search queries to find relevant videos and images from YouTube and Pexels
  • Provide time-aware responses: Detect your timezone to offer contextually appropriate greetings (e.g., "Good morning")
  • Ensure account security:
    • Track failed login attempts to prevent unauthorized access
    • Enforce password strength requirements
    • Prevent password reuse (last 3 passwords)
    • Manage session timeouts and automatic logouts
  • Prevent abuse and spam:
    • Rate limit login attempts, registrations, and password resets
    • Rate limit feedback submissions
    • Detect and block suspicious activity
    • Hash IP addresses for spam prevention
  • Ensure safety: Detect crisis situations and log concerning patterns for review
  • Send essential communications: Welcome emails, password reset links, and account notifications
  • Improve Snowfriend: Use anonymous, aggregated data to enhance the experience

AI Processing & Third-Party Services

How Your Conversations Are Processed:

  • Snowfriend uses AI language models provided by Groq to generate responses
  • Your conversation messages are sent to Groq's API for processing
  • Your first name is included in requests to personalize responses
  • If the primary service is unavailable, a local backup model on our own servers is used instead
  • We use industry-standard API security (HTTPS, authentication tokens)
  • No email address or full name is sent to AI providers

For more information about Groq's privacy practices, visit: groq.com/privacy-policy

Media Content Providers:

  • When you request videos or images, Snowfriend searches using YouTube Data API v3 and Pexels API
  • Your search queries (e.g., "healthy relationships advice") are sent to these services
  • Your name, email, and conversation history are NOT shared with media providers
  • Only the search query itself is transmitted
  • Media searches are subject to YouTube's and Pexels' respective privacy policies

External Privacy Policies:

  • YouTube Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Pexels Privacy Policy: pexels.com/privacy-policy

Email Communications

We send emails for essential account functions only:

  • Welcome Email: Sent when you create your account
  • Password Reset: Sent when you request to reset your password (link expires in 24 hours)
  • Security Alerts: In the future, we may notify you of suspicious account activity

Email Security:

  • All emails are sent via secure SMTP with TLS encryption
  • Email sending has a 10-second timeout for security
  • We use Gmail's SMTP server for reliable delivery
  • Email addresses are never shared with third parties

Account Security Features

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your account:

Password Security

  • Argon2 Encryption: Most secure password hashing algorithm available
  • Password Requirements: Minimum 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
  • Common Password Blocking: System prevents use of commonly compromised passwords
  • Password History: Prevents reuse of your last 3 passwords for enhanced security
  • Sequential Character Detection: Blocks passwords with predictable patterns (e.g., "12345", "abcde")

Login Protection

  • Failed Attempt Tracking: Maximum 5 failed login attempts allowed
  • Account Lockout: 15-minute lockout after maximum failed attempts
  • Dual Tracking: Monitors both IP address and account for suspicious activity
  • Auto-Clear: Failed attempt counter resets upon successful login

Session Management

  • Session Timeout: Automatic logout after 1 hour of inactivity
  • Browser Close: Sessions automatically expire when browser is closed
  • Password Reset: All active sessions invalidated when password is changed
  • Secure Cookies: HTTPS-only, HTTP-only, SameSite strict cookies

Rate Limiting & Anti-Abuse Measures

To prevent spam and abuse, we enforce the following limits:

Authentication Limits

  • Login: Maximum 5 attempts before 15-minute lockout
  • Registration: Maximum 3 accounts per IP per hour, 5 per day
  • Password Reset: Maximum 3 requests per IP per hour, 5 per email per day

Message Limits

  • Daily Limit: 15 messages per user per day
  • Reset Schedule: Message limits reset every hour on the hour (e.g., if you use all messages at 2:30 PM, you get 15 new messages at 3:00 PM)
  • Countdown Timer: Real-time countdown displays exactly when your messages will reset
  • Status Tracking: Message counter updates automatically showing remaining messages
  • Input Restrictions: Message input and send button are disabled when limit is reached

Feedback Limits

  • Per Minute: 1 submission maximum
  • Per Hour: 5 submissions maximum
  • Per Day: 3 submissions maximum

Suspicious Activity Detection

  • System automatically detects XSS, SQL injection, and command injection attempts
  • IP addresses generating 3+ suspicious patterns are blocked for 1 hour
  • All suspicious activity is logged for security review
  • Content sanitization removes potentially harmful code from all inputs

Time & Location Data

Timezone Detection:

  • Snowfriend detects your timezone to provide time-appropriate responses (e.g., "Good evening" instead of "Good morning")
  • Timezone is inferred from your device settings or IP address
  • No precise location data (GPS coordinates, street address, etc.) is collected or stored
  • Only general timezone information (e.g., "Asia/Manila") is used
  • This feature improves conversation quality by making responses contextually relevant

Data Security

Password Security: Passwords are hashed using Argon2, the most secure algorithm available. Previous password hashes (last 3) are stored encrypted to prevent password reuse.
HTTPS Encryption: Data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS
No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell or share your conversations with third parties
CSRF Protection: All forms are protected against cross-site request forgery attacks
Administrative Access Controls: Administrator access is restricted to whitelisted IP addresses only. All admin actions are logged with timestamps and IP addresses. Admin sessions timeout after 15 minutes (stricter than regular user sessions).
IP Address Hashing: IP addresses are hashed using SHA-256 with a unique salt for rate limiting and abuse prevention. Original IP addresses are not stored.

Logging & Monitoring

We maintain comprehensive security logs to protect your account and improve our services:

What We Log

  • Authentication Activity: Login attempts (successful and failed), registrations, password resets
  • Security Events: Suspicious activity, blocked IP addresses, failed validation attempts
  • Administrative Actions: Admin logins, user modifications, system changes (with full audit trail)
  • Crisis Detection: Flagged messages indicating potential self-harm or crisis (first 100 characters only)
  • Feedback Security: Suspicious feedback submissions, spam attempts

Information in Logs

  • Timestamps of events
  • Usernames (not full names or emails)
  • Hashed IP addresses (for security events)
  • Action types (login, logout, password change, etc.)
  • Success or failure status

Log Access

  • Logs are accessible only to authorized system administrators
  • All log access is itself logged for accountability
  • Logs are reviewed only for security incidents or system maintenance
  • No personally identifiable information is shared from logs

Feedback System Privacy & Security

Feedback submissions are designed to be anonymous while preventing abuse:

  • Anonymous by Design: No account required to submit feedback
  • IP Hashing: Your IP address is hashed using SHA-256 with a unique salt ("snowfriend_feedback_salt_2025") for spam prevention
  • Original IP Not Stored: Only the hashed version is kept
  • Image Processing: Uploaded images are re-encoded to strip all metadata (EXIF data, location, camera info)
  • Content Sanitization: All text is sanitized to remove potentially harmful code
  • Suspicious Content Flagging: Messages containing XSS, SQL injection, or other attack patterns are automatically flagged for review
  • Size Limits: Images max 5MB, messages max 1000 characters, total request max 10MB
  • Data Retention: Feedback is automatically deleted after 90 days (configurable)

Your Rights & Controls

You have the right to:

  • View Your Data: All conversations are visible in your chat interface
  • Delete Conversations: Clear your conversation history at any time using the "Clear" button
  • Delete Memory: Remove Snowfriend's memory of topics discussed using "Delete Conversations & Memories"
  • Export Conversations: Download your conversations as text files (includes both regular messages and media search results)
  • Change Password: Update your password at any time (previous 3 passwords cannot be reused)
  • Close Account: Contact us to delete your account and all associated data
  • Review Security Logs: Request information about security events related to your account

Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Conversations: Stored until you manually delete them
  • Account data: Retained as long as your account is active
  • Memory data: Stored until you clear it or delete your account
  • Password history: Last 3 password hashes stored (encrypted) until new passwords replace them
  • Security logs: Retained for security analysis and incident investigation (retention period varies by log type)
  • Failed login attempts: Cleared after successful login or after lockout period expires
  • Media search history: Stored in your user memory until cleared
  • Feedback submissions: Automatically deleted after 90 days
  • Deleted data: Permanently removed from our database immediately
  • Backup data: May persist in backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten

Crisis Detection & Logging

Important Safety Feature: When Snowfriend detects potential crisis situations (such as mentions of self-harm or suicide), the following information is logged:

  • Username (not full name or email)
  • Timestamp of concerning message
  • Category of concern detected
  • First 100 characters of the message

This logging helps us monitor for potential emergencies and improve our crisis detection system. Logs are stored securely and reviewed only when necessary for safety purposes.

Cookies & Session Management

We use essential cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in (session cookies - expire after 1 hour or when browser closes)
  • Protect against security threats (CSRF tokens)
  • Remember your authentication state

Cookie Security Settings:

  • HTTPS Only: Cookies are only transmitted over secure connections
  • HTTP Only: Cookies cannot be accessed by JavaScript
  • SameSite Strict: Cookies are not sent with cross-site requests
  • Session Timeout: Automatically expire after 1 hour of inactivity
  • Browser Close: Session cookies expire when browser is closed

We do NOT use:

  • Third-party tracking cookies
  • Analytics cookies that identify you personally
  • Advertising cookies
  • Social media tracking pixels
